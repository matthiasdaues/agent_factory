# Establish contract-owned testing policy

Turn the sustainable-testing proposal into the repository's concise new-test and
test-deletion convention.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- The convention defines the four layers, one-owner rule, new-test admission
  questions, equivalence-class guidance, and representative-fault deletion
  evidence.
- It rejects fixed coverage percentages, cosmetic case-count targets, and tests
  duplicating deterministic linters.
- `rules.md` contains only the canonical aphorisms and links to the convention.
- `index-lint --check` passes after regeneration.
