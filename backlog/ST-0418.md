# Retry a failing gate within the configured attempts

**Priority:** must-have

## Goal

A story whose gate keeps failing gets `gates.attempts` attempts on its tier and `gates.attempts_escalated` on the stronger tier, the first attempt included, and then stops as `failed`. Every story that uses up its attempts escalates, also two in one wave.

## Domain Rule

- A counted attempt has the failure class `acceptance_unmet` or `contradictory_evidence`. A failing gate records `acceptance_unmet` (BR-114).
- The default of both values is 3. `gates.attempts_escalated: 0` turns escalation off.
- `escalate` grants the next tier only when BR-115 holds; the limit of one escalation per wave goes.
- `re-dispatch` refuses when the counted attempts on the tier are used up and escalation is possible. After the User's fix, `re-dispatch` grants new attempts and appends an item with `tier` and `attempt_count` to `attempt_grants` (BR-116, OI-13).
- HasStopped decides when a `failed` story has stopped. A failed attempt with a retry left is no decision point.

## Outputs

- `dispatch_lib.py` holds the cap, HasStopped and `attempt_grants`.
- `escalate` and `re-dispatch` follow BR-115 and BR-116.
- `implementation-agent.md` spawns a fresh developer agent with the gate output per retry, on both ways.
- `dispatch-contract.md` lists `attempt_grants`.

## Demo Scenario

1. In an orchestrated run, ST-0350 fails `dependency-check` on each attempt. After three counted attempts, `dispatch re-dispatch ST-0350` refuses and `dispatch escalate ST-0350` grants the stronger tier.
2. After three more attempts, `dispatch status` shows ST-0350 `failed`.
3. ST-0351 in the same wave also uses up its attempts, and `dispatch escalate ST-0351` grants as well.
4. With `gates.attempts_escalated: 0`, `dispatch escalate ST-0352` refuses and names the setting.

## Demo Data

None. The tests write ledger entries with attempts of the classes `acceptance_unmet` and `scope_violation` directly through the ledger model.

## Boundaries

- `dispatch` enforces the cap; the implementation agent does not count attempts itself.
- A story on tier `strong` does not escalate.
- `packages/factory/INDEX.yaml` is regenerated in the same commit.
- Source edits go to `packages/factory/`, never to `.agent-factory/factory/`.
- No edit of the specification, the architecture, the QA strategy, or `docs/testing.yaml`. A needed change there becomes a row of `docs/spec/work/spec-feedback-<id>.md`.

## Evidence

- [ ] `escalate` refuses before and grants after the attempts on the tier are used; an attempt of another class does not count (RL-05-CT-01).
- [ ] With the defaults, a failing story ends `failed` after three attempts per tier; with `attempts_escalated: 0`, no attempt runs on a stronger tier (RL-05-CT-02).
- [ ] A story on `strong` fails after its attempts without escalating (RL-05-CT-03).
- [ ] Two stories of one wave both escalate (RL-05-CT-04).
- [ ] `escalate` refuses at `attempts_escalated: 0` (RL-05-CT-05).
- [ ] `re-dispatch` refuses when the attempts are used up and escalation is possible, and keeps the tier while attempts remain (RL-05-CT-06, RL-05-CT-07).
- [ ] After the User's fix, `re-dispatch` grants new attempts, also with escalation off, and records the grant (RL-06-CT-02).
- [ ] HasStopped is false for a failed attempt with a retry left (RL-03-CT-01).
- [ ] The implementation agent text records `acceptance_unmet`, spawns a fresh developer agent with the gate output, and applies the counts on both ways (RL-05-LN-01 to RL-05-LN-03).
