# Claim-reviewer agent

Author the claim-reviewer agent, which attempts to refute a claim. Its
definition states the checks it performs and the boundary that keeps it from
editing the claim it reviews.

The reviewer checks whether the claim can be falsified, whether the sources
support its exact wording, whether the sources are independent, whether
credible alternatives were considered, whether the tests were severe, whether
assumptions were added after a failed test, and whether the claim exceeds the
tested scope. It may not edit the claim.

**Priority:** must-have
