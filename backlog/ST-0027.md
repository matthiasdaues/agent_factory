# Researcher agent

Author the researcher agent, which researches a bounded question and forms
testable claims. Its definition states what it may do, what it must record,
and the boundary that keeps it from judging its own work.

The researcher may find sources, assess source provenance, record evidence,
propose testable claims, and design or execute refutation tests. It must record
supporting evidence, contrary evidence, source limitations, alternative
explanations, failed searches, and unresolved gaps. It may not review or vote
on its own claim.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- `factory/agents/researcher.md` lists the researcher's permitted actions, its
