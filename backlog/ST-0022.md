# Templates and schemas for claim-register and final-report

Author the paired template and JSON Schema for the two output artifacts: the
claim register and the final report.

The claim-register schema separates surviving, refuted, unresolved, and
superseded claims, and records for each surviving claim its text, scope,
assumptions, evidence, tests, failed tests, reviews, vote result,
qualifications, remaining possible refuters, and applicable date. The
final-report schema requires that every factual section reference one or more
surviving claim IDs and distinguishes surviving findings, refuted conjectures,
unresolved alternatives, recommendations, evidence gaps, and limitations.
Keeping failed tests and qualifications as first-class fields is what later
lets report validation prove they remain visible.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- Templates exist for claim-register and final-report with required headings
  and short field instructions, using the same field names as their schemas.
- The claim-register schema separates surviving, refuted, unresolved, and
  superseded claims and requires failed tests and qualifications per surviving
  claim.
- The final-report schema requires a surviving-claim-ID reference on each
  factual section and separate sections for findings, refuted conjectures,
  unresolved alternatives, recommendations, evidence gaps, and limitations.
- A negative test proves a report section without a surviving-claim reference
  fails `schema-validate`.
- A negative test proves a claim-register instance missing a required field
  fails `schema-validate`.
