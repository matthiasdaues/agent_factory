# Adversarial-review and research-reporting skills

Author the two output-stage skills as `SKILL.md` capability definitions.

Adversarial-review tests a claim, its evidence, and its refutation attempts,
producing a review and a vote against their templates and schemas, applying the
ten review checks and the defect classification. Research-reporting builds a
report from the frozen claim register without adding new findings, producing a
final report that preserves qualifications and cites surviving claim IDs.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- `adversarial-review/SKILL.md` describes applying the ten review checks,
  classifying defects, and producing a review and vote tied to an exact claim
  hash.
- `research-reporting/SKILL.md` describes building a report from the frozen
  claim register with no new findings, citing surviving claim IDs and keeping
  qualifications.
- Each skill states that it provides a capability and does not control
  sequence.
- Each skill carries the frontmatter `index-lint` requires (including a
  `category`) so it is discovered and grouped.
