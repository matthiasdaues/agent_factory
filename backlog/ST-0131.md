# risk_domains and strategy fields on story template with backlog-lint validation

Add two optional fields to the story template: `risk_domains` (closed enum:
security, privacy, data_integrity, compatibility, reliability, operations) and
`strategy` (closed enum: direct, seams-first; default: direct). Extend
`backlog-lint` to validate both fields against their enums, validate that
`seam_outputs` and `impl_outputs` are disjoint when present, and validate
that their union equals `outputs` for seams-first stories. Migrate existing
stories without these fields by adding defaults.

**As a** planning agent,
**I want** risk_domains and strategy as validated story fields,
**so that** the tier rubric and seams-first workflow can read them mechanically
from story frontmatter.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

### Story template

1. `story.md` template documents `risk_domains` as an optional array with a
   closed enum: security, privacy, data_integrity, compatibility, reliability,
   operations.
2. `story.md` template documents `strategy` as an optional string with a closed
   enum: direct, seams-first (default: direct).

### backlog-lint validation

3. `backlog-lint` rejects unknown `risk_domains` values (e.g., "securty").
4. `backlog-lint` rejects unknown `strategy` values (e.g., "seams").
5. `backlog-lint` rejects `seam_outputs` and `impl_outputs` that share a path.
6. `backlog-lint` rejects seams-first stories where the union of `seam_outputs`
   and `impl_outputs` does not equal `outputs`.

### Backlog migration

7. Existing stories without `risk_domains` or `strategy` are valid (fields are
   optional; absent means no risk domains and strategy: direct).

- Implement optional `risk_domains` and `strategy` frontmatter fields in the story template.
- Keep `strategy` defaulting to `direct` when absent, and treat absent `risk_domains` as no risk domains.
- Extend `factory/scripts/backlog-lint` to validate the new closed enums, reject overlapping `seam_outputs`/`impl_outputs`, and require seams-first output partitions to match `outputs`.
