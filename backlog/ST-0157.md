# Update developer-agent for test-harness mismatch feedback

Update `factory/agents/developer-agent.md` to add an explicit harness-mismatch check after writing tests and before reporting the story as complete. When the developer-agent encounters a mismatch between the QA strategy's prescribed layer or tooling and the repository's actual test harness, it invokes `spec-feedback` against the QA strategy document.

**Priority:** should-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- `factory/agents/developer-agent.md` includes a step at Step 5 (line 74) to check for harness mismatches
- When a mismatch is found (missing fixture pattern, no marker support, wrong entry point, missing infrastructure), the developer-agent invokes `spec-feedback` against the QA strategy document
- The finding names the contract, the prescribed layer, and the concrete obstacle
- The finding names the specific contract-owner row that is wrong and proposes a correction
- The QA strategy is updated in the same story or in a follow-up QA loop, not deferred indefinitely
- `mdformat` passes on the modified file

- This uses the existing `spec-feedback` mechanism; the change is an explicit workflow step
