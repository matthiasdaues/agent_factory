# Remove the complete Codex Factory footprint tracelessly

Extend the install manifest and remover to reverse skills, generated agents,
aliases, guardrail, and capture configuration while preserving project-owned
Codex content.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- The manifest records every Factory-owned `.agents` and `.codex` path plus
  whether `.codex/hooks.json` pre-existed.
- Removal strips exact Factory hook handlers from project-owned JSON, including
  guardrail and capture entries, and deletes a Factory-created hook file.
- Generated agents and linked skills/aliases are removed without following or
  deleting foreign paths.
- `.agents`, `.agents/skills`, `.codex`, and `.codex/hooks` are pruned only when
  empty.
- Fresh install/remove and project-content coexistence round trips are
  byte-identical.
