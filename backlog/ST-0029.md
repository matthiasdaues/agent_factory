# Research-report-writer agent

Author the research-report-writer agent, which writes the final report from the
frozen claim register. Its definition states what it may do and the boundaries
that keep it from introducing new findings or overstating survival.

The writer may arrange surviving claims, summarize them, and preserve
refutations and limitations. It must not conduct new research, create claims,
remove qualifications, present a surviving claim as proved, or use rejected or
unresolved claims as facts.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- `factory/agents/research-report-writer.md` lists the writer's permitted
  actions and every forbidden action as the proposal's "Research Report Writer"
  section states.
- The definition references the report policy.
- The agent carries the frontmatter `index-lint` requires so it is discovered
  and indexed.
