# Research-planning and source-research skills

Author the two planning-and-collection skills as `SKILL.md` capability
definitions.

Research-planning turns an approved brief into research questions, assignments,
competing conjectures, and stop conditions, producing a research plan against
the plan template and schema. Source-research finds sources for one bounded
assignment and records them against the source-record template and schema.
Both skills declare that they provide a capability and do not control the
workflow — the playbook does.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- `research-planning/SKILL.md` describes turning a validated brief into a
  research plan with questions, competing conjectures, assignments, review
  requirements, and stop conditions.
- `source-research/SKILL.md` describes finding and recording sources for one
  bounded assignment against the source-record artifact.
- Each skill states that it provides a capability and does not control
  sequence.
- Each skill carries the frontmatter `index-lint` requires (including a
  `category`) so it is discovered and grouped.
