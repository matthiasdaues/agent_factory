# Merge beginner content into factory-guide.md Getting Started section

Add a "Getting Started" section at the top of `factory/docs/factory-guide.md`,
before the existing "Agents" section. The content is adapted from
`docs/arc42/beginner-intro.md`. This is the foundational content that makes the
newcomer tour work in downstream projects where only `factory/` is installed.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- `factory/docs/factory-guide.md` opens with a "Getting Started" section
  containing these subsections: Who this is for; The one idea to hold onto;
  The four words you will keep hearing; Two ways to run it (manual mode only);
  Why two agents, not one; Your very first session; The bigger picture: five
  phases; Which playbook, when; Three habits that keep you safe; Where to go
  next.
- The "Graduating to automatic mode" section and all orchestrator references
  are removed.
- Links to `orchestrator/README.md` and `docs/arc42/concepts.md` are removed.
- Internal links are rewritten to `factory/`-relative paths.
- The guide's existing opening line ("If you are brand new, start with the
  beginner's introduction") is replaced with a direct lead-in to the new
  Getting Started section.
- A clear seam separates beginner and reference material: "Everything below
  is reference material. You don't need it yet."
- The walkthrough covers manual mode only.

## Implementation Notes

Read `docs/arc42/beginner-intro.md` for source content. Adapt, do not
copy verbatim — internal links must be rewritten and orchestrator content
stripped. Preserve the progressive structure: beginner content first,
reference material after the seam.
