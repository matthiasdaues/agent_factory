# Consolidate shared capture lifecycle and installation tests

Make the shared lifecycle suite own cross-adapter drain, cancellation, fencing,
and supervisor cleanup; keep installation and removal cases at their filesystem
boundary without repeating adapter behavior.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- Record before/after collected cases and lines for the three modules.
- Express equivalent native-adapter lifecycle outcomes once rather than as a
  full CLI-by-operation cross-product.
- Preserve distinct installation security, idempotency, capability-probe, and
  project-owned configuration boundaries.
- Preserve removal byte-identity and no-resurrection contracts.
- Demonstrate a representative lifecycle and install/remove fault is detected by
  the surviving owner.
- The affected modules and full suites pass with warnings treated as errors.
