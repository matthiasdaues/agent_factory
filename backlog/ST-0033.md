# Research-topic playbook wiring the 13-step procedure

Author the research-topic playbook, which runs the workflow from research
brief to validated final report. It wires the thirteen procedure steps in
order, naming the agent, the skills, the input artifacts, and the output
artifacts for each step, and it places the three-stage validation gate
(schema, then policy, then semantic) between steps so an output must pass
validation before the next step begins.

The playbook declares the brief as its input and the plan, assignments,
sources, conjectures, tests, reviews, votes, claim register, and final report
as its outputs. It enforces that at least two researchers work independently on
each conclusion-critical question and that at least three reviewers assess each
material claim, and it routes refuted or unresolved claims back through
resolution, where any semantic change starts a new claim version.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- `factory/playbooks/research-topic.md` defines all thirteen steps in
  order, each declaring its agent, its skills, its input artifacts, and its
  output artifacts.
- Between steps, the playbook runs schema validation, then policy validation,
  then semantic review, and blocks progression when a stage fails.
- The playbook declares the research brief as input and the full output set
  (plan, assignments, sources, conjectures, tests, reviews, votes, claim
  register, final report).
- The playbook enforces at least two independent researchers per
  conclusion-critical question and at least three reviewers per material claim,
  and routes failed claims through resolution with new-version semantics.
- The playbook carries the frontmatter `index-lint` requires so it is
  discovered and indexed.
