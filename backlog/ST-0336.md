# Record and look up domain terms in the glossary

**Priority:** must-have

## Goal

When `domain-modeling` settles a term, the term lands as a row with Term,
Definition, and Avoid in its section of `docs/spec/glossary.md`. The review
and interview skills look terms up there. No skill in this story writes or
reads a `CONTEXT.md` for terms any more.

## Domain Rule

- New terms land as rows of a Term/Definition/Avoid table in
  `docs/spec/glossary.md`, never in a `CONTEXT.md` (DEC-12).
- A glossary section covers one bounded context or one topic, such as
  acronyms (DEC-15). Which contexts exist is routing, and
  `docs/agent-context.md` holds it (DEC-07).
- A term appears at most once inside one section (CAL-G13, default below). A
  term may appear in more than one section.

## Outputs

- **Template.** `rulebooks/templates/context.md` becomes the glossary template
  `rulebooks/templates/glossary.md`: one `##` section per context or topic, each
  one table with the columns Term, Definition, and Avoid. Every link to the old
  template name points at the new one, the one link in `reverse-map/SKILL.md`
  included.
- **Convention.** `context-format.md` describes `docs/spec/glossary.md` and the
  domain concerns of `docs/agent-context.md`, and no longer describes
  `CONTEXT.md` or `CONTEXT-MAP.md`.
- **Writer.** `domain-modeling` adds or updates the row of a settled term in the
  section of its context, and creates the section when it is missing.
- **Readers.** `grilling`, `write-adr`, `process-transcript`, `inspect-spec`,
  `fagan-review`, `reconcile-spec`, `scratchpad`, and `proposal-review-agent`
  look terms up in `docs/spec/glossary.md`.
- **Text check.** `test_core_layout_references.py` gains the CAL-12-LN-01 check
  with its first assertions: `domain-modeling` writes table rows into
  `docs/spec/glossary.md` and names no `CONTEXT.md` as a term store.

## Demo Scenario

1. In the migrated fixture `glossary-project` (Demo Data), the maintainer runs
   `domain-modeling` and settles the term "Payout" for the context "Billing"
   with the definition "Money sent to a merchant after settlement" and the
   avoid word "Disbursement".
2. The maintainer opens `docs/spec/glossary.md`. The "Billing" table has a row
   `| Payout | Money sent to a merchant after settlement | Disbursement |`.
3. The maintainer runs `inspect-spec`. Its findings cite
   `docs/spec/glossary.md` for vocabulary.
