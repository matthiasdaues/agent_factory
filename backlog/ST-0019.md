# Templates and schemas for brief, plan, and assignment

Author the paired template and JSON Schema for the three planning-stage
research artifacts: the research brief (playbook input), the research plan,
and the research assignment.

The brief schema requires the fields the playbook input demands: research
question, intended use, audience, scope, exclusions, freshness requirements,
source requirements, cost of error, and completion criteria. The plan schema
covers research questions, competing conjectures, evidence requirements,
refutation strategies, assignments, review requirements, and stop conditions.
The assignment schema covers the bounded question and its assignment type
(direct-evidence, contrary-evidence, or alternative-explanation). Each
template uses the same field names as its schema and carries only headings
that have a current use.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- Templates exist for research-brief, research-plan, and research-assignment,
  each with required headings, short field instructions, and no unused
  optional sections.
- JSON Schemas exist for all three artifacts and validate required fields,
  types, allowed values, and identifier formats.
- Each template's field names match its schema's field names exactly.
- Templates contain no workflow rules (those live in policies and playbooks).
- A negative test proves an instance missing a required field fails
  `schema-validate` for each of the three schemas.
- The brief schema enforces every field the proposal's "Inputs" section
  requires of a valid brief.
