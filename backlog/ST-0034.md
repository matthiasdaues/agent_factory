# Regenerate INDEX and close out required-test coverage

Regenerate `factory/INDEX.yaml` with `index-lint` so the new research agents,
skills, playbook, and policy rulebooks appear in the catalog, and confirm that
every required test and every completion criterion from the proposal maps to an
accepted story.

The `policies/` category is discovered automatically by `index-lint`, which
derives a rulebook's category from its parent directory. The JSON Schemas under
`schemas/` are data validated by `schema-validate`, not prose rulebooks, and
`index-lint` scans only `.md` files, so schemas do not appear in the catalog by
design. This story records that decision and verifies the regenerated index is
clean.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- `factory/INDEX.yaml` is regenerated by `index-lint` and includes the four
  research agents, the six research skills, the research-topic playbook, and
  the four research policy rulebooks under the `policies` category.
- `index-lint` reports zero errors against the regenerated index.
- A coverage checklist maps each of the proposal's fifteen "Required Tests" to
  the story whose acceptance criteria prove it, with no test unmapped.
- A completion checklist confirms every item in the proposal's "Completion
  Criteria" is satisfied by an accepted story.
- The decision that `schemas/*.schema.json` are intentionally absent from the
  catalog is recorded, so a later reader does not treat the absence as a defect.
