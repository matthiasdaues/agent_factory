# Consolidate research artifact structure tests

Remove field-by-field and prose-fragment checks already owned by frontmatter,
schema, index, or higher-level policy validation. Preserve distinct behavioral
contracts and role-separation boundaries.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- Record before/after collected cases and lines for the declared files.
- Map retained cases to distinct contracts; identify the deterministic validator
  owning every removed structural assertion.
- Consolidate equivalent agent/skill/policy shape checks without introducing a
  new general-purpose test framework.
- Demonstrate a representative malformed artifact is rejected by the surviving
  owner.
- The research-domain and full suites pass with warnings treated as errors.

## Implementation evidence

Base verification used the full SHA
`0a6fe27bdc94d993b778676b6286f051d2fc5828` and reported zero commits ahead
of `feat/sustainable-testing` before implementation.

### Inventory

The nine declared test files collected **111 cases in 1,656 lines** before
consolidation and **62 cases in 1,215 lines** afterwards: 49 cases and 441
lines removed. Removed cases asserted only file existence, frontmatter keys or
values, index grouping metadata, or the existence of a policy already indexed
from its canonical path.

Deterministic ownership of those removed assertions is:

- `factory/scripts/index-lint` owns discovery, canonical paths, required
  agent/skill metadata, phase/category grouping, and referenced input paths.
- `factory/INDEX.yaml`, checked by `index-lint --check`, owns the generated
  inventory and detects missing or malformed indexed artifacts.
- Existing schema and policy validators continue to own research artifact
  structure; this story did not remove their behavioral validation.

### Retained contracts

- Agent tests retain permitted/forbidden actions, mandatory evidence records,
  author/reviewer separation, and required policy references.
- Skill tests retain capability boundaries, required research content,
  anti-sequencing rules, refutation readiness, adversarial checks, and the
  no-new-findings reporting constraint.
- Policy tests retain all role-conflict and claim-admission rules, decisive-vote
  semantics, evidence quality/corroboration rules, reporting qualifications,
  and explicit separation from schema and playbook concerns.

### Fault detection and verification

A temporary, uncommitted removal of `name: researcher` from
`factory/agents/researcher.md` made `factory/scripts/index-lint --check` exit 1
with `factory/INDEX.yaml is stale`. Restoring the line made the same validator
pass; `git status` confirmed the artifact itself was unchanged.

- Declared research suite: **62 passed** with warnings as errors.
- Full suite: **399 passed** with warnings as errors.
- `factory/scripts/index-lint --check`: passed after restoration.
