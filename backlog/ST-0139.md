# Phase 3 end-to-end smoke test: failure, escalation, and re-dispatch

## Analysis

Add one new end-to-end smoke test in `tests/test_dispatch_e2e.py` that reuses
the existing git-repo fixture helpers and drives the real `dispatch` CLI
through the Smoke 2 journey: initial dispatch, `acceptance_unmet` failure,
tracked evidence, `dispatch escalate`, `dispatch re-dispatch`, higher-tier
redispatch, merge, and wave closeout.

Planned coverage:

- `dispatch mark-failed` uses a tracked evidence file.
- Escalation is granted only after the failed attempt is recorded.
- Re-dispatch returns the story to `prepared`, then the retry runs at the
  escalated tier.
- Merge updates the backlog story to `status: done` and leaves the ledger in a
  terminal completed state.

Files to change:

- `tests/test_dispatch_e2e.py`
- `backlog/ST-0139.md`

Concern:

- The current dispatch implementation records failed attempts explicitly; the
  successful retry is observable through the escalated tier, second commit, and
  final merge rather than a second failure attempt entry.

Extend the end-to-end smoke test with a journey that exercises failure
classification, evidence-gated escalation, and class-aware re-dispatch. A story
fails with `acceptance_unmet`, is escalated one tier, re-dispatched at the
higher tier, and merges successfully. Asserts: two attempts in the ledger,
escalation recorded, story done, correct tier progression.

**As a** dispatch operator,
**I want** proof that the failure-escalation-re-dispatch cycle works end to end,
**so that** I can trust the Phase 3 mechanisms before using them in production.

**Priority:** should-have

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A story starts at economy tier, is dispatched, and fails with
   `acceptance_unmet` and tracked evidence.
2. `dispatch escalate` grants the escalation (economy -> standard).
3. `dispatch re-dispatch` succeeds after escalation.
4. The story is re-dispatched at standard tier, completes, and merges.
5. The ledger shows two attempts: the first failed at economy, the second
   succeeded at standard.
6. The escalation is recorded in the ledger.
7. The test runs in under 60 seconds.
