# Create comic-relief skill for VIRGIL

Create `factory/skills/comic-relief/SKILL.md` — a skill that produces
nerdy, goofy, context-aware humor. VIRGIL loads it when a moment of
levity is warranted: a long session that needs a breather, a gate that
just passed after three failures, a question so vague even grilling
can't save it, or a user who just deleted their own branch.

**Priority:** should-have

## Humor Profile

The tone lives in the quadrangle spanned by four vertices:

| Vertex         | What it contributes                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Dilbert**    | Corporate absurdity, process theater, meeting hell                                                                                                                                   |
| **XKCD**       | Nerd sniping, off-by-one jokes, "technically correct"                                                                                                                                |
| **South Park** | Irreverent escalation, nothing sacred, dark-but-quick                                                                                                                                |
| **Hornblower** | Bone-dry naval understatement. "The situation was not, perhaps, entirely satisfactory" after the third failed gate. The officer who describes a catastrophe as "somewhat irregular." |

Blend, don't imitate. The humor should feel like a colleague who reads
all four, not a parody of any one.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `factory/skills/comic-relief/SKILL.md` exists with valid skill
  frontmatter.
- The skill produces short-form humor: one-liners, fake error messages,
  commit-message suggestions nobody should use, or brief observational
  asides. Never more than two sentences.
- Output is context-aware — references the current situation (the
  playbook, the gate, the tool, the conversation) rather than canned
  jokes.
- Irony and sarcasm are allowed; mean-spiritedness is not. The target
  is the process, the tooling, or the absurdity of the situation —
  never the user.
- The skill is listed in VIRGIL's skill table with trigger: "when a
  moment of levity is warranted."
- VIRGIL's agent definition (`factory/agents/virgil.md`) includes
  `comic-relief` in its skill list.

## Implementation Notes

The skill is invoked by VIRGIL at its own discretion — there is no
explicit user trigger. VIRGIL judges timing: too often kills the joke,
too rarely wastes the skill. A good default is roughly once per
extended session, never during a gate failure that the user is actively
debugging, and never in generated artifacts (proposals, specs, commit
messages that will be merged).
