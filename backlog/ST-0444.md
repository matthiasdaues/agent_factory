# Resume a story blocked by a CRAP gate that cannot measure

After a person fixes the setup, `dispatch resume-gates` returns the story to the gate-check step with its code and attempts unchanged.

**Priority:** must-have

## Goal

`dispatch resume-gates ST-0350` sets a story that is `blocked` with the reason `gate cannot measure: crap-score` back to `dispatched`. It keeps the attempts, the branch and the worktree, and starts no developer agent, so the next step is the gate-check step. It refuses every other story and names its state and reason.

## Analysis

- `dispatch_lib.py` gains the edge from `blocked` to `dispatched`, the constant `GATE_CANNOT_MEASURE_REASON`, and the guard `resume_gates_refusal`. `resume-gates` joins `STOP_SUBCOMMANDS`.
- `dispatch` gains `resume-gates`. `mark-dispatched` now refuses every state but `dispatching`, because the new edge would otherwise let it skip the guard of BR-135.
- `implementation-agent.md` names `resume-gates` in the command table, step 4d, the stops by hand, the refusal before any gate (DEC-58), and the section on a gate that cannot measure (DEC-56). `dispatch-contract.md` describes the subcommand.
- Tests cover the rows CG-09-CT-01, CG-09-IT-01 to CG-09-IT-03, CG-09-LN-01, CG-04-LN-02, CG-11-LN-03, CG-08-LN-01, CG-10-CT-01, CG-08-E2-01, and RL-06-CT-01.

## Domain Rule

- The ledger gains the edge ResumeGates from `blocked` to `dispatched`. Its guard accepts only `blocked` with the exact reason, for a story or a finding (BR-135, DEC-22).
- `resume-gates` keeps `attempts`, `attempt_grants`, the branch head and the worktree, and writes no manifest and no brief (DEC-07).
- It refuses `blocked` with another reason, `dispatched`, and `failed` with exit 1, names the state and the reason, and leaves the entry unchanged.
- During a stop, it exits 1 and prints the stop notice.
- `dispatch re-dispatch` stays the way back for a story whose code must change, and sets `prepared`.
- The implementation agent runs `resume-gates` and then the gate-check step after the User says to continue, with no spawn.
- A refused merge goes to the fix-iteration loop only after exit 1. After exit 3, the story is `blocked`, and step 4d of `implementation-agent.md` leads to `resume-gates`.
- A `crap-score` run stopped by `SIGTERM`, `SIGINT` or `SIGHUP` exits 128 plus the signal number and gives no verdict. The gate-check step runs it again and records no attempt (DEC-56).
- The refusal of a story that names `crap-score` without an entry records no attempt, because no developer agent can fix a missing setup (DEC-58).

## Demo Scenario

1. ST-0350 is `blocked` with the reason `gate cannot measure: crap-score` and one attempt, as in ST-0443.
2. The User fixes the ready stub and tells the implementation agent to continue. The agent runs `dispatch resume-gates ST-0350`.
3. `dispatch status` shows ST-0350 `dispatched` with one attempt, the head of `story/ST-0350` is unchanged, and no developer agent starts.
4. The gate-check step passes, and `dispatch merge-story ST-0350` merges the story.
5. `dispatch resume-gates ST-0351` on a story `blocked` with `merge conflict` exits 1 and names `blocked` and `merge conflict`.
