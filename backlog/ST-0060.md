# Define survey brief, plan, and report contracts

Add `mode` to the shared research brief, defaulting to `survey`, and define
dedicated survey-plan and survey-report artifacts. Survey findings cite source
records directly; they do not impersonate falsification findings with empty
claim, test, vote, or register fields.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- The research brief accepts only `survey` or `falsification` and treats an
  omitted mode as `survey`.
- The survey plan requires research questions, search angles, source targets,
  bounded assignments, and stop conditions.
- Every survey report finding requires at least one source-record reference.
- The survey report requires uncertainties, evidence gaps, limitations, and
  candidates for deeper falsification study.
- Existing falsification artifacts still validate unchanged.
- Schema tests cover valid, missing, malformed, and cross-mode artifact cases.

## Implementation Notes

Keep the falsification plan and final-report schemas unchanged. The survey
contracts are separate because their factual basis is recorded sources, not a
frozen claim register.

## Analysis

Implement the survey research artifact contracts described in the Survey/Synthesis
Research Mode design specification. The shared research brief will gain an optional
`mode` field constrained to `survey` and `falsification`; omitted mode remains
valid and means `survey` to consumers. New, separate survey-plan and survey-report
schemas and matching templates will model source-grounded survey work without
claim-register, test, or vote fields.

The work will modify `research-brief.schema.json` and its template; create the two
survey schemas and templates; and create `test_research_survey_artifacts.py`.
Tests will exercise the production `schema-validate` CLI with valid, missing,
malformed, and cross-mode fixtures. They will also prove the existing
falsification plan and final-report contracts still accept their valid artifacts.

The seam is `factory/scripts/schema-validate <artifact> <schema>`, the same
deterministic validation boundary used by research consumers. No use-case files
exist for the backlog trace labels, so the design specification supplies the
behavioural contract. `mode` defaults semantically rather than by mutation: JSON
Schema validation accepts an omitted field while its `default` documents the
consumer default.
