# Define survey brief, plan, and report contracts

Add `mode` to the shared research brief, defaulting to `survey`, and define
dedicated survey-plan and survey-report artifacts. Survey findings cite source
records directly; they do not impersonate falsification findings with empty
claim, test, vote, or register fields.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- The research brief accepts only `survey` or `falsification` and treats an
  omitted mode as `survey`.
- The survey plan requires research questions, search angles, source targets,
  bounded assignments, and stop conditions.
- Every survey report finding requires at least one source-record reference.
- The survey report requires uncertainties, evidence gaps, limitations, and
  candidates for deeper falsification study.
- Existing falsification artifacts still validate unchanged.
- Schema tests cover valid, missing, malformed, and cross-mode artifact cases.

## Implementation Notes

Keep the falsification plan and final-report schemas unchanged. The survey
contracts are separate because their factual basis is recorded sources, not a
frozen claim register.
