# Add "file as backlog story" destination to retrospective action-item tracking

`factory/skills/retrospective/SKILL.md` Step 3 ("Extract action items") and
`factory/agents/coaching-agent.md` Step 3 ("Track action items") both list
the tracking destinations a confirmed retrospective action item can be filed
to. Today those are: `docs/spec/todo.md`/`docs/spec/todos.md`, the project's
issue tracker, an agent/skill update, or `docs/CONTEXT.md`. Neither file
offers "file as a backlog story and hand it to `implementation-agent`" as an
option — this session's retrospective (2026-07-10) needed exactly that path
for its four confirmed action items, and had to improvise it because no such
destination existed.

**Priority:** should-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- `factory/skills/retrospective/SKILL.md` Step 3's destination list (the two
  bullets under "Confirmed items should be:") gains a third bullet: filed as
  a backlog story (`backlog/ST-NNNN.md`, this repo's INVEST/MoSCoW schema)
  and handed off to `implementation-agent` for dispatch.
- `factory/agents/coaching-agent.md` Step 3's parenthetical destination list
  (`docs/spec/todos.md` as a new T-NNN, issue tracker, agent/skill update, or
  `docs/CONTEXT.md`) gains the same fifth option, worded consistently with
  the other four.
- Neither file's Completion Criteria section needs new wording — "every item
  has a tracking location" already covers the new option.
- No other step or section of either file is restructured.
