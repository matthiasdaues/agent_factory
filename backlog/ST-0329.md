# Keep folded concerns through a rescan

**Priority:** must-have

## Goal

The project maintainer runs `capture-context --update --scan` on a project with
folded concerns and keeps every one of them. The rescan offers no removal of a
concern that carries the fold mark, also when it is not a scope-map area.

## Contract

| Operation                         | Input state                                                      | Outcome                                | Persistence                                        |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `capture-context --update --scan` | Domain concern "Ledger" with the fold mark, not a scope-map area | No removal offered; the concern stays  | `docs/agent-context.md` unchanged for that concern |
| `capture-context --update --scan` | Domain concern "Archive" without the mark, not a scope-map area  | Removal offered as today               | As the developer decides                           |
| `capture-context --update --scan` | Folded concern whose `Read:` path moved                          | Path change offered; the concern stays | As the developer decides                           |

## Demo Scenario

1. The maintainer takes the fixture `folding-project` of ST-0328 after its fold,
   adds the unmarked domain concern "Archive", and runs
   `capture-context --update --scan`.
2. The diff lists "Archive" as a possible removal and does not list "Billing",
   "Ledger", or "Shipping".
3. The maintainer declines the removal. All four concerns stay in
   `docs/agent-context.md`.

## Demo Data

No new seeded data. The demo uses `folding-project` of ST-0328 after the fold,
plus one unmarked domain concern "Archive" added by hand.

## Boundaries

- The rescan never offers to remove a folded concern.
- The three-way diff of `--update --scan` keeps its other rules.
