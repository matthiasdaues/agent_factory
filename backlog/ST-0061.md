# Add survey planning and synthesis capabilities

Teach research planning to produce the artifact required by the brief's mode,
and add a Research Synthesizer that turns recorded survey sources into a cited
synthesis. Preserve the Research Report Writer's frozen-claim-register
boundary.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- Research planning selects the survey-plan contract for survey briefs and the
  existing research-plan contract for falsification briefs.
- The Research Synthesizer writes only findings supported by cited source
  records and preserves uncertainty, gaps, and limitations.
- Survey synthesis does not use claim-survival, admission, refutation, or proof
  language.
- The existing Research Report Writer and `research-reporting` skill remain
  restricted to frozen claim registers.
- New agent and skill frontmatter pass index validation.

## Implementation Notes

Do not overload `research-reporting`: its prohibition on adding findings beyond
a frozen register is a useful falsification invariant.

## Analysis

Implement mode-aware guidance in `research-planning`: an omitted or explicit
`survey` brief selects `research-survey-plan.schema.json` and its template,
whereas a `falsification` brief retains the existing research-plan contract.
Create a separate `research-synthesis` capability and `research-synthesizer`
agent to turn only recorded source records into a survey-report artifact. Both
will require citations for every finding and retain uncertainties, evidence
gaps, limitations, and candidates for deeper falsification study. They will
not use claim admission, survival, refutation, or proof framing.

Files to modify or create: `factory/skills/research-planning/SKILL.md`,
`factory/skills/research-synthesis/SKILL.md`,
`factory/agents/research-synthesizer.md`, and
`orchestrator/tests/test_research_survey_capabilities.py`. The story metadata
will be updated to `done`; the generated Factory index will be refreshed if
its validator requires it.

Tests will cover the Survey Mode / Research Planning choice of plan contract,
the Survey Synthesis source-record citation and uncertainty requirements, the
ban on falsification-only language in synthesis guidance, and the unchanged
frozen-claim-register boundary of the Research Report Writer and
`research-reporting` skill. The public seams are the skill/agent Markdown
contracts and the Factory discovery index; this follows the existing
capability-definition test boundary.

No implementation ambiguity remains: the ST-0060 schemas and templates define
the survey artifact shapes, while the existing report writer remains wholly
falsification-specific.
