# Add survey planning and synthesis capabilities

Teach research planning to produce the artifact required by the brief's mode,
and add a Research Synthesizer that turns recorded survey sources into a cited
synthesis. Preserve the Research Report Writer's frozen-claim-register
boundary.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- Research planning selects the survey-plan contract for survey briefs and the
  existing research-plan contract for falsification briefs.
- The Research Synthesizer writes only findings supported by cited source
  records and preserves uncertainty, gaps, and limitations.
- Survey synthesis does not use claim-survival, admission, refutation, or proof
  language.
- The existing Research Report Writer and `research-reporting` skill remain
  restricted to frozen claim registers.
- New agent and skill frontmatter pass index validation.

## Implementation Notes

Do not overload `research-reporting`: its prohibition on adding findings beyond
a frozen register is a useful falsification invariant.
