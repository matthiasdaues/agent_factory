# Dispatch ledger subcommands for state transitions and wave closure

Wire the argparse stubs for `mark-dispatching`, `mark-dispatched`,
`mark-blocked`, `mark-failed` (basic, no failure-class vocabulary),
`re-dispatch` (basic, state transition only), and `close-wave` into working
subcommand handlers. Each handler reads the ledger, applies the state
transition through the validated state machine from ST-0086, writes the
ledger back, and exits zero on success, non-zero on invalid transition.

**As a** dispatch operator,
**I want** subcommands that transition story states through the validated
lifecycle,
**so that** the ledger always reflects the current dispatch state and invalid
transitions are rejected mechanically.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

### State-transition subcommands

1. `dispatch mark-dispatching <story-id>` transitions a prepared story to
   dispatching. Non-prepared stories are rejected.
2. `dispatch mark-dispatched <story-id>` transitions a dispatching story to
   dispatched. Non-dispatching stories are rejected.
3. `dispatch mark-blocked <story-id> --reason <text>` records a blocking
   condition on any non-terminal story.
4. `dispatch mark-failed <story-id>` (Phase 1 basic) transitions a dispatched
   or dispatching story to failed.
5. `dispatch re-dispatch <story-id>` (Phase 1 basic) transitions a failed or
   blocked story back to prepared. Non-failed/non-blocked stories are rejected.

### Wave closure

6. `dispatch close-wave <N>` exits zero when all stories in wave N are terminal
   (done, blocked, or failed), appends a closeout record, and commits.
7. `dispatch close-wave <N>` exits non-zero when any story in wave N is
   non-terminal.

### Idempotency

8. Re-running any subcommand that already succeeded is a no-op (exit zero, no
   duplicate ledger entries).

- "Spawn failure from dispatching transitions to failed"
- "Record a blocking condition"
- "Re-dispatch validates story is in failed or blocked state"
- "Blocked story can be re-dispatched after operator resolves the condition"

From **Feature: Wave Lifecycle** (Bounded Context 1):

- "All stories terminal allows wave closure"
- "Non-terminal story blocks wave closure"
