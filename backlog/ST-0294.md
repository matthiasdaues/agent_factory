# Conduct a moderated usability session and record comprehension

**Priority:** could-have

## Goal

A quality researcher conducts a moderated usability session with a participant who did not build Agent Factory. The protocol template at `docs/testing/value-first-onboarding-usability-protocol.md` guides the session through the complete onboarding journey and records elapsed time, decision count, unclear terms, abandonment points, recovery attempts, and the participant's stated next action.

## Domain Rule

- The participant did not build Agent Factory.
- The protocol covers the complete journey including the first task.
- The recording fields capture verbatim participant language for unclear terms and stated next action.
- The moderator does not intervene or guide the participant except when the participant is stuck and asks for help.

## Boundaries

- This story produces no production code. It produces one document template.
- The protocol template must not contain Factory-internal terminology without a plain-English definition.

## Affected Paths

- `docs/testing/` — new `value-first-onboarding-usability-protocol.md`.

## Demo Scenario

01. Open `docs/testing/value-first-onboarding-usability-protocol.md`.
02. Verify the document contains sections for session metadata (date, participant identifier, moderator), journey phases (installation, first session, first task), and recording fields.

05. Record the number of decisions the participant made.
06. Record any terms the participant found unclear.
07. Record any points where the participant abandoned a step.
08. Record any recovery attempts the participant made after an error.
09. Record the participant's stated next action after completing the session.
