# Wire the survey playbook and mode front gate

Add a five-step survey playbook and route survey briefs to it at the research
front gate. Keep `research-topic.md` as the thirteen-step falsification
playbook.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- `research-survey.md` defines validation, planning, source gathering,
  synthesis, and report validation in that order.
- Omitted or explicit `survey` mode selects the survey playbook; explicit
  `falsification` selects `research-topic.md`.
- Every survey artifact passes schema, policy where applicable, and semantic
  validation before progression.
- Survey report validation proves that every finding references a recorded
  source and uses no falsification-only status language.
- The Factory guide explains both modes and the escalation path from survey
  findings to a later falsification brief.

## Analysis

Implement the research front gate as a prose contract shared by the
orchestrator and the two sibling playbooks. An omitted mode or `survey` routes
to the five-step survey workflow; only explicit `falsification` enters the
existing thirteen-step workflow. The survey workflow will retain the
CLI-neutral assignment contract and sequential source-gathering fallback added
by ST-0063.

Files changed:

- `factory/playbooks/research-survey.md` — define the ordered five-step survey
  procedure and its schema → policy-where-applicable → semantic gates.
- `factory/playbooks/research-topic.md` — add the front-gate routing contract
  without changing its thirteen falsification steps.
- `factory/agents/research-orchestrator.md` — make mode selection, survey
  progression, and final report checks explicit role responsibilities.
- `factory/docs/factory-guide.md` — document both modes and escalation from
  survey findings to a separate falsification brief.
- `orchestrator/tests/test_research_survey_playbook.py` — exercise the public
  Markdown contracts at their highest-level seams.
- `factory/INDEX.yaml` — regenerate catalog metadata after playbook and agent
  descriptions change, if required by `index-lint`.

The tests cover Survey Playbook ordering, Mode Selection for omitted and
explicit modes, the Validation Gate on every survey artifact, source-reference
and status-language report validation, preservation of the thirteen-step
falsification workflow, and guide-level escalation instructions.

No ambiguity requires a new architecture decision: the sibling-playbook
structure, default mode, dedicated survey contracts, and CLI-portable dispatch
behaviour are already fixed by the survey-mode design and predecessor stories.
