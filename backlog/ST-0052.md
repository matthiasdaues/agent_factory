# Thin native CLI adapter smoke suites

Limit Claude, Copilot, and Codex end-to-end tests to adapter-specific payload,
accounting, wiring, and one representative failure path. Shared persistence and
lifecycle behavior belong to their shared owners.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- Record before/after collected cases and lines for all three adapter modules.
- Each CLI retains a happy path, its distinct accounting semantic, and only
  adapter-specific failure/wiring coverage.
- Remove repeated shared-core and lifecycle assertions with links/comments
  identifying their surviving owner.
- Demonstrate one malformed adapter payload is still caught or handled as the
  adapter contract requires.
- The adapter and full suites pass with warnings treated as errors.
