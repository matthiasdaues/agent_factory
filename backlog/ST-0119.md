# dispatch prepare-story: serial chain link preparation

Implement `dispatch prepare-story <story-id>` for lazily preparing serial chain
links after their predecessor merges. The subcommand verifies the chain
predecessor is `done`, cuts the story branch from the predecessor's merge
commit, creates the worktree, runs `verify-base` against the merge commit, and
records `prepared` in the ledger.

**As a** dispatch operator,
**I want** serial chain links to be prepared automatically from their
predecessor's merge commit,
**so that** dependent stories start from the correct base without manual branch
management.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `dispatch prepare-story <story-id>` exits non-zero unless the chain
   predecessor is `done`.
2. The story branch is cut from the predecessor's merge commit.
3. `verify-base` runs against the predecessor's merge commit.
4. The story is recorded as `prepared` in the ledger.
5. Re-running after success is a no-op.
