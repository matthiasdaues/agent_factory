# Prove a portable survey run end to end

Exercise one bounded landscape question from a mode-defaulted brief through a
validated cited synthesis, using the same canonical artifacts across every
supported CLI discovery surface.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- The fixture run validates its brief, survey plan, source records, and survey
  report through the declared gates.
- Repeated or related sources remain distinguishable by source family.
- Every finding resolves to at least one source record, and unsupported source
  references fail deterministically.
- The run creates no conjecture, test, review, vote, or claim-register artifact.
- Installed discovery exposes the required survey playbook, skills, and agents
  under Claude Code, Copilot, Codex, and Pi.
- The complete existing falsification research test suite remains green.
