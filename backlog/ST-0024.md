# Author evidence and report policies

Write the evidence and report policies as prose rulebooks under
`factory/rulebooks/conventions/research-`.

The evidence policy requires precise source references, source dates, source
provenance, source-family identification, source limitations, contrary-evidence
searches, and a clear separation between evidence and interpretation. It states
that several copies of one source count as one source family and that
repetition is not independent corroboration. The report policy requires the
report to rest on surviving claims, cite claim IDs, preserve material
qualifications, show important failed or inconclusive tests, distinguish
findings from recommendations, identify unresolved questions, and avoid
language that presents claims as proved. It records the preferred wording:
"survived the defined tests", "not refuted within the tested scope",
"provisionally retained", and "remains open to refutation".

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- `evidence-policy.md` states every evidence requirement from the proposal and
  the rule that copies of one source form a single source family that does not
  corroborate itself.
- `report-policy.md` states every report requirement and lists the preferred
  non-proof wording.
- Both documents carry frontmatter that `index-lint` can read under the
  `policies` category.
- The documents describe rules only; they contain no schema definitions and no
  step ordering.
