# Write JSON-lines audit entries

Every step writes one JSON-lines entry to `.current-work/audit.log`. Fields: timestamp, playbook, state, agent, action, trigger_exit, phase_advance_exit, phase_retry_exit, iteration, duration_seconds.

## Acceptance Criteria

- After a successful advance, an audit entry with action=advance is appended.
- After a halt, an audit entry with action=halt is appended.
- After a human-gate stop, an audit entry with action=human-gate is appended.
- After completion, an audit entry with action=done is appended.
- Entries are valid JSON, one per line, parseable with `jq`.
