# Remove run-all and ChainRunner (deferred scope)

Automated full-chain execution and unattended autonomy are deferred (NG6): the `run-all` command, the `ChainRunner` sequencer, the `--yes` / auto-approve flag, and the Scheduler path are removed from the codebase. The Operator drives the chain one phase at a time via `run-phase` (UC-03); every run is human-attended. These capabilities return with a messaging channel or Web-UI (T-36) and should not be reintroduced piecemeal before then.

**Priority:** must-have

## Acceptance Criteria

- `src/orchestrator/chain_runner.py` is removed, along with its `test_chain_runner.py`.
- The `run-all` subcommand and its handler are removed from `cli.py`; a bare unknown `run-all` prints help and exits non-zero like any unknown command.
- The `--yes` / `--auto-approve` flag and its auto-approve dispatch are removed; `resume` continues the current phase directly rather than through a chain sequencer.
- The `--interactive` flag becomes `--no-interactive` (the interactive default's opt-out); default resolution is interactive with a TTY, headless without one.
- `test_cli.py` and any other tests are updated to drop run-all / `--yes` cases; the full suite passes.
- No dangling references to `ChainRunner` or `run-all` remain in `src/` or `tests/`.
