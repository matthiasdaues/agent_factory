# UC-10, UC-11, and UC-12 have no Postconditions section, so the requirements gate fails

**What is wrong:** UC-10, UC-11, and UC-12 document their guarantees under `## Minimal Guarantees` and `## Success Guarantees` headings and never use a `## Postconditions` heading. UC-08 and UC-09 use `## Postconditions`. `spec-lint` requires each use case to carry a Postconditions section and reports three `STRUCT001` errors, so the deterministic requirements gate exits non-zero and the phase cannot pass. The split also breaks Cockburn-template consistency across the use-case set.

**Fix:** Add a `## Postconditions` heading to UC-10, UC-11, and UC-12, grouping the existing Minimal Guarantee and Success Guarantee content beneath it exactly as UC-08 and UC-09 do. Re-run `spec-lint` and confirm zero errors.
