# Model resolution wrongly combined agent tier and story classification via a "higher-of-two elevation"

**What is wrong:** The resolution of SPEC-0003 (and ADR-0018) settled the model-selection rule on a "two-axis elevation" — during the implementation phase, the story's classification would elevate the agent's effective tier, "the higher of the two wins." That framing is a category error. During implementation the `implementation-agent` acts as a **dispatcher** that commissions **developer sub-agents**, and those developer agents declare **no tier** by design: the model for a unit of work is the dispatcher's decision, made from the story's `classification` alone, below the adapter boundary (FR-M). There is no agent tier to take the maximum with, so the "higher of the two wins" rule is meaningless at the level where it was said to apply.

The wrong rule had propagated to six artifacts: `cli_specification.md` (design rule 4 and the resolution-chain box), `prd-tui-addendum.md` (FR-R12 and the T-34 resolution note), `validation-rules.md` (VR-023, and VR-041's null-tier scope), `adr/0018`, `08_crosscutting_concepts.md` (§8.12), and `04_solution_strategy.md`. The defect surfaced during planning, when story ST-0049 could not be written coherently against it.

**Fix (applied):** The two axes are restated as operating at **two levels that never combine**. Agent tier governs every invocation the orchestrator makes directly (`run-step`'s named agent; each phase author/reviewer in `run-phase`/`run-all`). Story classification is the sole determinant of a developer sub-agent's model, chosen by the dispatcher during implementation from the classification alone; developer agents are tier-less. VR-041's null-tier `standard` fallback is scoped to orchestrator-invoked agents only. All six artifacts, plus the DSL `ModelResolver` description and the chapter 5 component row, were corrected; T-34's resolution note was redone. The backlog was split accordingly into ST-0049 (agent-tier resolution) and ST-0059 (classification-driven dispatcher selection). `spec-lint` and `arch-lint` remain at 0 errors.
