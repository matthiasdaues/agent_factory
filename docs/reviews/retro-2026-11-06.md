## ✅ Went Well

- **The reviews caught real defects before anything reached `dev`.** Each one came with a reproduction or a concrete failure:
  - SEC-0007: `next-id` printed a token from the remote URL. The stakeholder called it "a very good catch".
  - IMPL-0009: a resumed move handed out `ST-0013` twice in a scratch repository.
  - FAGAN-0033: step 4 could start a second counter.
  - BUG-0116: the BUG-0115 fix broke `dispatch init`.
  - IMPL-0004: a claim overrode the user's ssh command.
  - ATAM-0014: an interrupted move could lose `C`.
- **Agents stopped at their stop conditions instead of working around them.** That way the real causes surfaced:
  - ST-0368 stopped on the test that pinned chapter 5 at 54 components, a regression from `13736117`.
  - ST-0373 stopped on two broken anchors in the proposal.
  - ST-0383 and ST-0384 stopped on two ST-0352 tests that forbid `RECON`, which led to DEC-15.
  - ST-0376 stopped on the missing `scope:` in the proposal.
- **The mechanical gates caught scope errors.** `premerge-check` blocked two merges: ST-0373, because the proposal lay outside its `touches:`, and ST-0374, because 29 stray SVG files sat in its commit.
- **The counter dogfooded itself without a collision.** After the seed, every ID came from `next-id`: SPEC-0059 to SPEC-0070, ATAM-0014 to ATAM-0017, ST-0376 to ST-0385, ADR-0033, T-0402 to T-0404, and BUG-0118 and BUG-0119. No number came out twice.
- **Grilling one question at a time, each with a concrete case, settled decisions quickly.** counter-ids-fitting got eleven decisions in eight questions, most of them answered with one letter.
- **The finding-classification rules kept minor findings from looping.** About 30 minor findings went into the registers. Only `major` findings blocked, and every review closed after at most four passes.
- **T-0019 is solved at its root.** `arch-lint` re-rendered every diagram whenever `architecture.dsl` was newer than the SVG files. `62beeb74` adds `--no-export`. After that, the suite took 65 s instead of 141 s, and not one SVG changed again.

## 🛑 Stop Doing

- **Asserting a rule or a mechanism without reading it.** I made this mistake four times: the reopen rule for CID-G01, the scope source of `merge-story`, the BR-108 row for SPEC-0059, and the export condition of T-0019. Each cost a round, and the user corrected one of them. Evidence: the friction table above.
- **Briefs that allow broad staging or extra suite runs.** "Stage what a hook rewrites" led to `git add -A` with 29 SVG files. Running "the suite before the commit" led to two suite runs per commit, in parallel. Evidence: ST-0374 and the machine load in W2.
- **Suggesting commands whose output can carry a secret into the session.** My advice to use `! git remote get-url origin` would have shown the token. Evidence: the correction after the SEC-0007 decision.

## ▶️ Continue Doing

- **Findings to the stakeholder first, with a concrete case and a recommendation; the decision is theirs.** It works because the stakeholder decides in one word. Evidence: B for RECON, B for DEC-16, A for option A, and "strip the title" for DEC-12.
- **Reviewers check with a reproduction, not by reading alone.** It works because it proves a defect beyond doubt. Evidence: IMPL-0009 with `ST-0013` twice, FAGAN-0033 with `ST-0013` and `ST-0042`, and SEC-0007 with a made-up token.
- **The dispatcher commits when several agents work in the same worktree.** It works because no agent takes another agent's file along. Evidence: the drift rows of ST-0381 and IMPL-0008 after `37b87979`.
- **At most two developer agents at a time, and test-heavy work in sequence.** It works because the machine stays responsive. Evidence: W2 of counter-ids, and all five waves of counter-ids-fitting.
- **Checking every agent claim against git.** It works because the reports were not always right. Evidence: the SHA checks, the file list before `--max-files 76`, and the outdated remark about SPEC-0060.
- **Recording decisions and todos at once, with IDs from `next-id`.** It works because nothing gets lost across three compactions. Evidence: DEC-24 and DEC-25 in counter-ids, DEC-01 to DEC-16 in counter-ids-fitting, and T-0402 to T-0404.

## 🆕 Start Doing

- **A fixed brief template for developer agents.** It holds these rules:

  - stage by name only, never `git add -A`;
  - run tests only through the hook, and only for the red and the green step;
  - restore the SVG files;
  - never print the remote URL;
  - use only made-up tokens.

  Emerged from: ST-0374, the machine load, and IMPL-0010. To adopt it, put it into `developer-agent.md` or `dispatch-contract.md`, not into the dispatcher's head.

- **A frontmatter gate for draft proposals.** `scope-lint` should run when a proposal is committed, not only in the test suite. Emerged from: the missing `scope:` that broke CAL-14 on every commit.

- **A `dispatch` command that records a new branch head.** It would replace the edits to the ledger by hand after a merge outside `merge-story`. Emerged from: `BASE_STALE` before W3 of counter-ids, and the edits to the ledger in counter-ids-fitting.

- **A guardrail hook that parses commands, not text.** Emerged from: the harmless blocks and the missed pytest call (T-0017).

- **A cap on test parallelism when agents run at once.** Emerged from: the machine load (the rest of T-0019).

- **Read the mechanism before suggesting a fix.** To adopt it, take one look at the code, the playbook, or the rule before every recommendation that names a mechanism. Emerged from: the four friction rows under Stop Doing.

## 🔍 Wave Dispatch in Detail

The stakeholder asked for a closer look at the wave dispatch. Across the two
runs there were 15 waves. The faults fall into five groups, and most share
one core: the ledger knows only what happens through `dispatch`, while much
happened beside it.

### 1. The ledger loses sight of the truth

| What happened                                                                                                                                            | Cause                                                                                                                      | Improvement                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `dispatch init` in the feature worktree wrote the ledger nested, at `.current-work/feature/X/.current-work/feature/X/`, so every call needed `--ledger`. | `init` derives its path from the worktree it runs in; the default lookup derives it from the main checkout.                | Both use one function: always `<main checkout>/.current-work/<feature branch>/dispatch-ledger.yaml` (T-0407).                                                       |
| `BASE_STALE` before waves 2 and 3, and three edits to the ledger by hand.                                                                                | Merges of `dev`, of fix branches, and of drift commits moved the feature branch. Only `merge-story` updates `branch_head`. | Don't store `branch_head`. Read it from git on every call; a read value cannot go stale.                                                                            |
| The fix branches for IMPL-0004 to IMPL-0010, BUG-0118, DEC-16, and FAGAN-0033 ran outside the ledger, with `premerge-check` and the merge by hand.       | `dispatch` knows only backlog stories.                                                                                     | `dispatch prepare-fix <finding>` and `merge-fix`, with the same worktree, `verify-base`, `premerge-check`, and post-merge suite as a story, recorded in the ledger. |

### 2. Two copies of the story file

### 3. Story branches with work in progress

| What happened                                                                                                                          | Cause                                                                                                            | Improvement                                                                                                                                                    |

### Smaller points

- `mark-dispatching` and `mark-dispatched` around every agent are a ritual; one `dispatch start <story>` that returns the brief is enough.
- The ST-0375 and BUG-0118 agents were resumed after their merge; `merge-story` could tell such an agent that its worktree is gone.
- The guardrail accepts worktree paths only relative, starting with `.current-work/`, from the main checkout, which is not obvious from inside a worktree.

### Where to start

Three changes would have prevented most of the friction: read `branch_head`
from git, let `merge-story` run every gate itself, and keep one source for the
story file, `dev`. Next come `prepare-fix` and `merge-fix`, `dispatch drift`,
and `refresh-story --keep-work`. Together they would have removed about 30
manual steps across the two runs. They touch `dispatch`, `premerge-check`, the
dispatch contract, and the implementation agent, so they belong in a proposal
that can absorb T-0014, T-0405, T-0407, and the open part of T-0019.
