## Correctness

**Story workflow.** The section `## Story workflow` lists Implement, Review, Fix, Gate-check step and Merge in the order of the Scenario and of the proposal. The Fix step asks for a repeat review until no blocking finding with the story's ID is open, and it links the blocking rule of `finding-classification.md`. Step 4b of the orchestrated workflow and step 4 of the run by hand both send the review to steps 2 and 3. The developer agent's new section `Place in the story workflow` names the same five steps and links the section.

**Stops by hand.** The table has one row for each row of the Scenario Outline. The text widens "after its last counted attempt on the tier strong" to "its counted attempts are used up and `dispatch escalate` refuses it". That matches HasStopped in the Feature background and BR-116, which name every reason of BR-115. A failed attempt with a retry left and the wave close are no stops, as the Scenario and the proposal say. The merge stop shows the output of `merge-story --dry-run`, as ADR-0037 asks.

**Who runs each command.** The table gives the User only `return-story`, `close --drop` and `close`, and gives `refresh-story` no use by hand. That matches the proposal's subcommand table and RL-C07. The table also lists `init`, `plan`, `prepare-wave` and `close-wave`, which the proposal table leaves out. These additions are correct.

**Developer agent.** Step 4 "Run the gates" is gone, and the Commit step says the agent commits on both ways and leaves `done` to `merge-story` (BR-118). Steps 5 and 6 became 4 and 5, and the step reference inside step 5 changed with them.

**Gate keys.** `implementation-agent.md` now names `gates.crap-score.threshold` and `gates.mutation-testing.*`. `GATE_KEY_TEXTS` lists the file, so RL-09-LN-01 checks it. Field keys such as `timeout_minutes` keep their underscores, as BR-055 says.

**rules.md.** The review checked each removal against the proposal's section Documents that change:

- The sentence that the human owns every commit, push and gate decision by hand goes, as the proposal says. Both ways now use the dispatch scripts and the ledger.
- The ledger rule goes. The proposal drops "the rule that the ledger records gate results and the merge commit as an audit trail". BR-111 also removes `gate_results` from the ledger, so the removed rule was wrong as well.
- The `(automated only)` markers of the Dispatch section go. Each of those rules holds on both ways now: SHA checks, terminal states per wave, recorded outcomes and closed dispatches.
- The status rule now leaves `status: done` to `merge-story` (BR-118).

Two markers outside the Dispatch section stayed. IMPL-0034 records them.

**branching-policy.md.** The new section `Story Branches In A Run By Hand` matches the Scenario "prepare-story checks out the story branch in the primary checkout" and BR-113.
