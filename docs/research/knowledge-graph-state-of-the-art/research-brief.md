# Research Brief: Knowledge Graph State Of The Art

This brief is the readable copy of [research-brief.json](research-brief.json).
The JSON file is authoritative and passes `research-brief.schema.json`.

The survey asks two questions. What is the state of the art in knowledge
graphs as of 2026? Which knowledge graph concepts went through several
documented rounds of formulation, criticism, and reformulation?

The survey grounds the design of a knowledge graph with provenance for the
Agent Factory. The earlier improvised round is in
[knowledge-graph-with-provenance](../knowledge-graph-with-provenance/synthesis.md).
The survey reports what sources document. A verdict that a concept survived
refutation belongs to a later falsification study.
