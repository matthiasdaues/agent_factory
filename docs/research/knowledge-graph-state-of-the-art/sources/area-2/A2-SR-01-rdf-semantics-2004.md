## Evidence

- Section 3.3.1: the meaning of a reification is that a document exists containing a triple token. The text says it does not interpret reification as a form of quotation.
- Section 3.3.1: "A reification of a triple does not entail the triple, and is not entailed by it." The reification only says that the triple token exists and what it is about, not that it is true.
- The page notes that RDF 1.1 Semantics supersedes this version.
