## Evidence

- Abstract: Cypher 9 is "the first version of the language governed by the openCypher Implementers Group"; the goal is that Cypher "will mature into an industry standard language for property graph querying".
- Introduction: "Given the lack of a standard for the language ... it has become pressing to agree on the formal data model" and semantics.
- Cypher 10 will be "a compositional language supporting graph projections and multiple named graphs".
- Text on multiple graphs: this parallels the LDBC G-CORE proposal; the two differ because G-CORE outputs only a single graph, while Cypher 10 is "closed under table-graphs".
- "In late 2015 Neo4j announced the openCypher project".
