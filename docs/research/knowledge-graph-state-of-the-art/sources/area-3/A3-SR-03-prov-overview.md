## Evidence

- Quote: "The design of PROV stems from the recommendations of the Provenance Incubator Group."
- Paraphrase: the Incubator report defined eight broad recommendations, and PROV supports all eight, directly or through extensibility points.
- The family has twelve documents. Four are Recommendations: PROV-O (an OWL 2 ontology that maps PROV to RDF), PROV-DM (the conceptual data model), PROV-N (a human-readable notation) and PROV-CONSTRAINTS (rules for valid provenance). The others are Notes: PROV-OVERVIEW, PROV-PRIMER, PROV-XML, PROV-AQ, PROV-DICTIONARY, PROV-DC, PROV-SEM and PROV-LINKS.
