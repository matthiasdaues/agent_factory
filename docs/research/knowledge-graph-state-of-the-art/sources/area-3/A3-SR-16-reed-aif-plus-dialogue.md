## Evidence

- Abstract: "This paper extends the Argument Interchange Format to enable it to represent dialogic argumentation." Two analogies carry the extension: "locutions in a dialogue are analogous to AIF I-nodes which capture propositional data" and "steps between locutions are analogous to AIF S-nodes which capture inferential movement."
- Section 1: "The AIF to date has had significant impact in practical development projects such as the WWAW [9], but in its 2006 form had not integrated modelling of dialogue."
- Section 1: "This has meant that one important strand of research (spanning philosophy, linguistics, natural language engineering and multi-agent systems) has been excluded from the benefits that the AIF affords."
- Section 1, goals: extend the AIF to represent "argumentation protocols" and "dialogue histories", place "little or no restriction on the types of dialogue protocol", and meet this "with the minimum extra representational machinery possible".

## What the source shows

It shows a third AIF formulation (2008): the 2006 model extended to dialogue, with an explicit statement of what the 2006 form left out.

## What the source does not show

It does not show an implementation, any RDF or OWL form of AIF+, or later criticism of AIF+.
