# Research Brief: Knowledge Across Sources

This brief is the readable copy of [research-brief.json](research-brief.json).
The JSON file is authoritative and passes `research-brief.schema.json`.

The survey widens the knowledge graph with provenance from the Agent Factory's
own documents to a personal or team knowledge system over news, email, chat,
and conversation transcripts, on any topic. The factory is the system's first
tenant and the method that builds and runs it. The earlier rounds are in
[knowledge-graph-with-provenance](../knowledge-graph-with-provenance/case-for-the-knowledge-graph.md)
and [knowledge-graph-state-of-the-art](../knowledge-graph-state-of-the-art/survey-report.md).

The survey covers four areas:

1. Personal and team knowledge management, from the Memex to current note
   graphs.
2. Claims across news: claim detection, fact-checking, ClaimReview,
   corrections, and retractions.
3. Email, chat, and meeting transcripts as sources.
4. Claim propagation and framing. A wire report and fifty outlets that reuse
   it are 51 sources. The wire is the primary source of the claim. Each outlet
   is a source of what it did with the claim: verbatim reuse, alteration,
   reframing, or independent reporting.

Every source counts as storable. Copyright and data protection are out of
scope; rule-based exclusions and deletion come later.

As before, the survey reports what sources document. A verdict that a concept
survived refutation belongs to a later falsification study.
