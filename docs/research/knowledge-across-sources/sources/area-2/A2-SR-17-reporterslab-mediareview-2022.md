## Evidence

- "MediaReview is a sibling to ClaimReview, an initiative the Reporters’ Lab led starting in 2015, that sought to create infrastructure for fact-checkers to make their articles machine-readable and easily used for search engines, mobile apps, and other projects."
- "Used by 177 fact-checking organizations around the world, ClaimReview has been used to tag 136,744 articles".
- "But ClaimReview proved insufficient to address the new, specific challenges presented by misinformation spread through multimedia."
- "The vast majority of media-based misinformation is rated “Missing Context,” or, as we’ve defined it, “presenting unaltered media in an inaccurate manner.”"
- "only 67% of submitted archive links were viewable on the chosen archive when accessed at a later date, while 33% were not."
- Shows: how fact-checkers used ClaimReview and MediaReview, and the stated reason for the second type. Does not show: an independent count of ClaimReview use.
