## Evidence

- Abstract: "It consists of 185,445 claims generated by altering sentences extracted from Wikipedia and subsequently verified without knowledge of the sentence they were derived from. The claims are classified as SUPPORTED, REFUTED or NOTENOUGHINFO by annotators achieving 0.6841 in Fleiss κ." The small capitals of the labels come out of pdftotext with spaces; they are joined here.
- Section 3.1: "we specified six types of mutation: paraphrasing, negation, substitution of an entity/relation with a similar/dissimilar one, and making the claim more general/specific."
- Section 3.1: "During trials of the annotation task, we discovered that the majority of annotators had difficulty generating non-trivial negation mutations".
- Appendix A: "Claim A claim is a single sentence expressing information (true or mutated) about a single aspect of one target entity."
- Shows: the three-label verification task with sentence-level evidence, built from synthetic claims. Does not show: how systems behave on claims from news or politics.
