### 4.1 Section 3 prose

Section 3 states no count or rank that the histories do not hold. It states one cause that the history does not hold. Section 3.9 says CheckThat! 2020 split the label "because of 'the subjective nature of check-worthiness'". In `check-worthy-claim.json` that criticism has `drove_next: false`, because Full Fact sits between the two CheckThat! steps. A2-SR-08 does present the subjectivity as the reason, so the sentence is supported by the record but not by the history.

Section 3 also lists three records that are primary for their own work but secondary for an earlier formulation. A1-SR-03 is a fourth. Its `source_family` field calls it secondary for the dated Xanadu designs, and `link-direction.json` step 2 rests on it for the 1960 design.

Section 3.10 says the 2016 and 2018 steps "carry its dates". In the history, the 2016 step carries 2016-01-01 and the 2018 step carries 2025-01-01. The sentence is true for the record's two dates, but it hides that the 2018 step is dated 2025.
