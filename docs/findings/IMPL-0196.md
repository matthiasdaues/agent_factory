# The gates page promises retries after a story has stopped

**What is wrong:** A reader can expect another retry after a gate fails on the last allowed attempt. The replacement sentence in the gates page says every failed gate is retried and says the implementation-agent definition sets the limits. The linked workflow ends the loop when the story has stopped and reads the limits from `docs/testing.yaml` (`packages/factory/agents/implementation-agent.md:311-328`).

**Why this severity:** The sentence gives the wrong behavior at the retry boundary and restates a binding rule in ordinary prose. That fails the handbook review check for BR-149.

**Fix:** Replace the retry claim and the claim about who sets the limits with a direct link to the implementation agent's attempts and escalation workflow.
