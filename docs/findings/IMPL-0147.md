# The authoring pages state binding rules in ordinary prose

**What is wrong:** A handbook author encounters binding instructions about rule placement and stub structure in ordinary prose. The fenced example in `page-format.md` illustrates a separate Markdown-formatting rule. The reference page also teaches the authoring procedure after its lookup tables, outside the proposal’s remit for a reference.

**Why this severity:** The QA strategy classifies binding rules in ordinary prose as fails-purpose defects. Its type check also limits reference pages to lookup material. Both pages are new on the story branch.

**Fix:** Put binding requirements in sourced rule summaries. Keep the reference focused on fields and page types, and place the worked authoring example in the contributing guidance.

## Repeat pass

The reference now ends after the lookup tables. The remaining defect starts in the stub guide at lines 17 and 24. An author still reads the one-sentence and source-link requirement and the in-place edit requirement as binding instructions in ordinary prose.

The new `quote-a-rule.md` repeats the placement rule in ordinary prose at line 11. Line 25 also tells authors to present a rule without a rulebook home as an explanation. An author can follow that instruction and restate a binding rule in ordinary prose.

Keep the worked example. Describe verified behavior as behavior. Route any binding requirement without a rulebook home to its owner before presenting it as a rule.
