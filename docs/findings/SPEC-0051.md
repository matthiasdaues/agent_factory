# Four Rules name actors that do not match their Scenarios or the glossary

**What is wrong:** The actor of a Rule should be the one who wants its outcome and who acts in its Scenarios.

- CID-07 names "Factory producer" as the actor, which is a file. Its Scenario Outline has a factory maintainer read the file.
- CID-08 and CID-09 name the CLI-Invoked Agent as the actor with its own goal. The glossary says a CLI-Invoked Agent "has no independent goal beyond executing the step it was handed". Their Scenario Outlines again have a factory maintainer read a file.
- CID-10 names the script `init-factory` as the actor. The User runs it and reads its message.

A planner who cuts stories by actor and observable outcome cannot tell from these Rules who sees the result.

**Fix:** Name the actor who sees each outcome. CID-07 to CID-09 describe text that a reader of the Factory files follows, so name that reader as the actor, with the term settled under SPEC-0050. CID-10 has the User as its actor. Update the Actor-Goal Matrix of the gaps report to match.
