# The seed accepts values that BR-070 and the claim commit contract cannot hold

**What is wrong:** BR-069 has two holes.

1. It accepts `9999` as `<number>` and as `<n>`. Then `next-id --seed 9999` makes the first claim of every prefix `10000`, and `ADR=9999` does the same for ADRs. BR-070 and gap CID-G04 cover only a prefix line that reaches `9999` through claims, not a seed at `9999`.
2. It does not forbid two pairs for one prefix. `next-id --seed 1000 ST=5 ST=7` would write two `ST` lines. The claim commit contract allows at most one line per prefix, and the spec does not say which line a claim then raises.

**Fix:** Refuse a second pair for the same prefix in BR-069, name the prefix in the message, and add the case to the seed Scenario Outline and the seed error table. Extend CID-G04 and BR-070 to a seed at `9999`. Alternatively, ask the stakeholder whether the seed range ends at `9998`.

**Resolution (pass 2, 2026-11-05):** BR-069 accepts 0 to 9998 and refuses a second pair for one prefix. BR-070, the entity model, and the seed error table match. SPEC-0056 covers the Scenario Outline rows and the missing record of the change against the proposal. SPEC-0055 covers the QA strategy.
