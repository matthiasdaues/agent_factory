# Story dependencies are never filled

**What is wrong:** `init` and `plan` do not copy dependencies from the story frontmatter. `next_ready` therefore lists a story whose dependency is still pending, and `prepare-story` always reports "has no chain predecessor recorded". Even with dependencies filled, `next_ready` counts a failed or blocked dependency as satisfied. Source: [bug-hunt report § D-4](../reviews/bug-hunt-2026-10-15.md#d-4--story-dependencies-are-never-filled).

**Fix:** Copy the dependencies from the frontmatter, and require them to be done.
