# CC-3 omits CONTEXT.md from brownfield-lite exit condition

**What is wrong:** Completion Criterion 3 states "Brownfield onboarding can exit
after Stage 1 with a valid architecture.dsl and scope-map.md." The Scope section
states the feature-addition prerequisites are relaxed to "architecture.dsl,
scope-map.md, and CONTEXT.md exist." CONTEXT.md is required for the
feature-addition entry but absent from the criterion that gates Stage 1 exit. A
planning agent cannot determine whether CONTEXT.md is a Stage 1 deliverable or
not.

**Fix:** Add CONTEXT.md to CC-3: "...with a valid architecture.dsl,
scope-map.md, and CONTEXT.md."
