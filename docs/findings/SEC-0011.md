# A story can switch off its own CRAP gate in `docs/testing.yaml`

**What is wrong:** A story can merge without a CRAP verdict. The story only has to set `gates.crap-score.enabled: false` in its own `docs/testing.yaml`, or remove the entry. Take `ST-0350`, which names `docs/testing.yaml` in `touches` because it adds a measure. If it also sets `enabled: false`, `premerge-check` runs no `crap-score` at the merge into the feature branch. It prints only the not-set-up notice of DEC-33, and the notice never blocks. The threshold, the patterns and the register are trusted values since ST-0450 (DEC-67), but the switch that decides whether the gate runs at all is not.

The problem starts in `premerge-check`. `resolve_gates` and `not_set_up_notice` read the gate list from `docs/testing.yaml` in the temporary worktree at the story head. `_picked_gates` then keeps only entries with `enabled: true`. DEC-75 and the contract § `crap-score` Reads keep `enabled` at the story head, so the code follows the specification. On 2026-11-23 the stakeholder decided that this is wrong, because a story must not switch off its own gate.

```text
ST-0350 sets gates.crap-score.enabled: false, or removes gates.crap-score, at its head
        ↓
premerge-check adds its worktree at the story head and calls resolve_gates(worktree, ST-0350)
        ↓
gate_entries reads docs/testing.yaml of the worktree, and _picked_gates drops crap-score
        ↓
check_semantic_gates runs no crap-score, and the merge passes with a notice that never blocks
```

The review ran `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-matthiasdaues-Documents-datenschoenheit-agent-factory/89fd1670-49e4-4a59-97db-e1035150aa04/scratchpad/qa/probe-enabled.py`. It calls `resolve_gates` and `not_set_up_notice` of `premerge-check` as lines 1502 and 1503 do, on three story heads:

```text
== story head enabled: true: gates ['crap-score']; notice ['SKIP not-set-up gates: docs/testing.yaml has no entry for mutation-testing (set up by the skill mutation-testing)']
== story head enabled: false: gates []; notice ['SKIP not-set-up gates: docs/testing.yaml has no entry for mutation-testing (set up by the skill mutation-testing)']
== story head no entry: gates []; notice ['SKIP not-set-up gates: docs/testing.yaml has no entry for crap-score (set up by the skill setup-crap-score) or mutation-testing (set up by the skill mutation-testing)']
```

**Why this severity:** A story that switches the gate off merges a function above the threshold through `premerge-check`, which is a gate bypass, so the bar starts at High. The attack needs write access to the story branch (`trusted_access`), which lowers it to Medium, and Medium gives `major`. The lines of `premerge-check` are unchanged on `dev`, so they are in use.

**Fix:** In `premerge-check`, the layer of the merge checks: read `enabled` of `gates.crap-score`, and whether the entry exists, from `docs/testing.yaml` at the tip of the integration branch. Use the branch that `crap-score` reads, as the key `integration_branch` of the `--base` revision names it. A story head that differs from the branch changes nothing. The story's `quality-gates` field still adds the gate. Keep `command` and `ready` of the measures at the story head (DEC-75). In the specification, change DEC-75 in `docs/proposals/crap-gate-setup.md` and the row Reads of § `crap-score` in `docs/spec/interface-contracts.md`, and name the source of `enabled` in the gate list of BR-066. Add an integration test in which a story head with `enabled: false`, and one without the entry, still get the verdict of `crap-score`.
