# The story commit changes `docs/spec/work/todo.md`, so `dispatch merge-story ST-0450` refuses the merge

**What is wrong:** The User who runs `dispatch merge-story ST-0450` gets a refusal from the scope check, and the story does not merge. The change is outside the subject of the story and looks unintended. The two proposals exist only as untracked files in the primary checkout, so the links are broken on the feature branch, and the developer probably fixed them while a hook ran. That repair belongs to a change of its own.
