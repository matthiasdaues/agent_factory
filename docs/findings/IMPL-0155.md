# The newcomer tour can skip a checkpoint confirmation

**What is wrong:** A newcomer can miss the chance to ask questions after a tutorial step. Step 04 lets the tour combine adjacent steps. Step 06 tells it to pause after each step. The tutorial gives adjacent steps their own checkpoints.

**Why this severity:** The onboarding scenario requires a pause after every section. The new instruction permits the tour to skip one.

**Fix:** Remove the exception in step 04. Keep step 06 and walk each tutorial checkpoint in order.
