# The proof worktree misses the uncommitted setup files

**What is wrong:** `gate-setup-proof` runs `crap-score` in a temporary worktree with two commits, and `crap-score` reads `docs/testing.yaml` of that folder (BR-127). Setup step 4 writes the entry with `enabled: false`, and the skill writes the measuring command, but no step commits either. A worktree cut from `HEAD` then has no entry and no measuring command, so `crap-score` exits 2 in every check. In step 7 the worktree also misses the new threshold. BR-138 and `docs/spec/interface-contracts.md` § gate-setup-proof do not say how these files reach the worktree.

**Why this severity:** An implementer who cuts the worktree from `HEAD` builds a proof that fails every real setup run, while the fixture tests of the QA strategy commit the entry and pass (fails_purpose). No story or code outside the run encodes the statements.

**Fix:** State in BR-138, BR-139 and § gate-setup-proof that the first commit of the proof worktree holds `docs/testing.yaml` and the files that the measure names as `command` and `ready` as they are in the working tree, plus `base/`, and that the setup commits once at the end (DEC-32).
