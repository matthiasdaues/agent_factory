# One cut-off character drops a whole capture

**What is wrong:** A strict UTF-8 read fails on a final line cut in the middle of a character. The stated rule is to skip bad lines. Source: [bug-hunt report § U-7](../reviews/bug-hunt-2026-10-15.md#u-7--one-cut-off-character-drops-a-whole-capture).

**Fix:** Decode with `errors="replace"`.

**Resolution:** Fixed in commit `08caf5b1bf3ffe94e79853a3a3c1be8886e4f392`, merged into `dev` as `3921e522cc31f7fd454d9c908c9e8e42ceb201fe`. The full suite passed after the merge.
