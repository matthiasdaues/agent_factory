# Crash recovery --clear --force not assigned to any subcommand

**What is wrong:** The Design Details section states: "A stale step manifest (agent died without cleanup) blocks subsequent writes. `--clear --force` removes it with a warning." No subcommand in the Subcommands section accepts `--clear` or `--force` flags. You cannot invoke crash recovery through any documented interface. The step manifest's no-supersede rule means a stale manifest permanently blocks the worktree until you manually delete the file, bypassing the dispatch script entirely -- which defeats the purpose of script-owned state management.

**Fix:** Assign the crash-recovery mechanism to a specific subcommand. Options: (a) add a `dispatch clear-manifest --force` subcommand that removes the stale manifest with a warning and records the recovery in the ledger, (b) add `--clear-manifest --force` as flags on `dispatch prepare-wave` and `dispatch prepare-story`, or (c) make manifest cleanup part of a general `dispatch recover` subcommand. Whichever is chosen, add it to the Subcommands list, its ledger behavior, and the Completion Criteria.
