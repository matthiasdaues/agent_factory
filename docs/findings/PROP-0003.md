# impact.boundaries under-declares the proposal's own Scope

**Resolution (verified 2026-08-18, repeat pass v2):** `impact.boundaries` now
lists all thirteen artifacts the Scope modifies — the three playbooks, five
agents (planning, developer, implementation, architecture, requirements),
create-backlog, validate, the story template, rules.md, and backlog-lint.
Creation targets (docs/charter/, charter templates, the two skills,
charter-lint) were optional per the original fix direction.

**What is wrong:** The frontmatter `impact.boundaries` lists seven paths, but
the proposal's own "In the first release" list modifies artifacts missing from
it: `factory/agents/implementation-agent.md` ("schedule Epic 0 as wave 1"),
`factory/agents/architecture-agent.md` (invokes `update-charter`, may read the
charter on remediation passes), `factory/scripts/backlog-lint` (validates
`tests:`), `factory/rulebooks/rules.md` (new Coding-section rules), and the
`validate` skill (runs `charter-lint`). The proposal template defines `impact`
as the declared reach of the change, and feature-addition Step 3.3 reconciles
the plan against the declared impact — an under-declared boundary list makes
that reconciliation miss a third of the change surface and understates the
review perimeter.

**Original fix direction (applied):** Extend `impact.boundaries` to cover every artifact the Scope modifies
or creates: the three agents (planning, developer, implementation,
architecture), `backlog-lint`, `rules.md`, the `validate` skill, and
(optionally, as create-targets) the `docs/charter/` directory and the
`charter-*` templates. Keep the list in lock-step with the Scope section.
