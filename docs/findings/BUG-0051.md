# `mermaid-lint` skips tilde fences and indented fences

**What is wrong:** A `~~~mermaid` block, or a fence indented inside a list item, passes even with raw semicolons. Source: [bug-hunt report § L-9](../reviews/bug-hunt-2026-10-15.md#l-9--mermaid-lint-skips-tilde-fences-and-indented-fences).

**Fix:** Match both fence characters and indented fences.

**Resolution:** Fixed in commit `c0d3611d4afb7ac7439747f08abf7eaef4e79539`, merged into `dev` as `3921e522cc31f7fd454d9c908c9e8e42ceb201fe`. The full suite passed after the merge.
