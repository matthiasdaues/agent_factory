# The authoring pages state binding rules in ordinary prose

**What is wrong:** Handbook authors still see binding requirements presented as ordinary instructions. In `stub-rule.md:17,24`, the numbered steps state that a stub carries one scope sentence and a source link and that authors fill it in place. Those steps cite the proposal. In `quote-a-rule.md:11`, a paragraph states where binding rules live and how handbook pages repeat them. It cites BR-149. The revised sentences still prescribe the same actions, and BR-149 requires a rule-summary callout with a source line.

**Why this severity:** ST-0456 requires every handbook page to keep binding rules out of ordinary prose. The QA strategy treats a violation as a fails-purpose defect. This finding reopens IMPL-0147.

**Fix:** Replace the rule guide's restatement with a pointer to BR-149. Where the stub guide needs to state a binding requirement, use a sourced rule summary once the requirement has a rulebook home. Keep the worked procedure and route any missing rulebook entry to its owner.
