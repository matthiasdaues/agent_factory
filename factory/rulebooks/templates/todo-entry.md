# Todo Entry Template

Skeleton for one entry appended to `docs/spec/todo.md`. Governed by [todo-format.md](../conventions/todo-format.md).

```markdown
## T-0001 — <short title>

- status: open
- source: <skill, session, or review that raised it> (optional)
- traces: <related ID(s) — story, finding, ADR> (optional)

<body: what is deferred, why, what resolving it requires>
```
